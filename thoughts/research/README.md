# Research

## Curriculum research: moved

Per-chapter research now lives INSIDE each chapter folder as `research.md`, not here. This gives one source of truth per chapter.

Example: `curriculum/multan-board/class-10/computer-science/ch1-operating-systems/research.md`

## What still goes in this folder

Non-chapter research only:
- Cross-chapter or cross-subject explorations
- Web research notes not tied to a specific chapter
- Design experiments not committed to a specific deck

If you find yourself writing chapter-specific research here, stop and move it to the chapter's own `research.md` instead.
