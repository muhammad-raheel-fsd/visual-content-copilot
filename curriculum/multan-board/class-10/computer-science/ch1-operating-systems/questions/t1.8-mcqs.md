## Q1

**Question:** According to the book, why are there different types of operating systems?

**Options:**
- a) Because each brand wants its own OS
- b) Because operating systems are designed according to the needs of the device and the work it performs
- c) Because programmers get bored of one design
- d) Because hardware changes every year

**Correct:** b

**Explanation:** From the book: "Operating systems are designed according to the needs of the device and the work it performs. Each type serves a different purpose and is optimized for specific tasks."

**Difficulty:** easy

**Bloom:** understand
