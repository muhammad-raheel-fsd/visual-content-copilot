(`videos/source-clips/meme-and-sound-assets/`, gitignored), each trimmed to its punchline and
loudness-equalised. These are clips from films, TV and creators: **they are not ours and carry
no licence**. Short meme use is common practice, but any of them can draw a copyright claim on
YouTube; if a claim lands, swap that file and re-render.

section 3), short (under 3 s), and the line must fit what was just said.
